# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project, whether in issues, pull requests or
discussions, is expected to make it a welcoming place.

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep the discussion about the project.
- Don't harass, insult or attack anyone, and don't discriminate against anyone.
- Don't share other people's private information, including phone numbers and screenshots of chats.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block anyone who
doesn't follow this code.

To report a problem privately, email the maintainer at shehawey9@gmail.com.
Reports are kept confidential.
